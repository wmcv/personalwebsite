Books

Zero to One

startups

Peter Thiel with Blake Masters

Useful for thinking about startups through differentiation rather than imitation. The strongest parts are its ideas around monopolies, contrarian thinking, and building something genuinely new.

The Mountain Is You

self-development

Brianna Wiest

Useful for recognizing self-sabotaging patterns and understanding how internal resistance can become the main obstacle to progress. It is more reflective than tactical.

Zen Mind, Beginner's Mind

zen

Shunryu Suzuki

A calm introduction to Zen practice and the value of approaching life without rigid assumptions. It is especially useful for thinking about attention, simplicity, and being present.

Can't Hurt Me

discipline

David Goggins

Useful for building discipline and learning how much perceived limits are shaped by discomfort and habit. The value is less in copying Goggins directly and more in adopting stronger personal accountability.

Value Investing: From Graham to Buffett and Beyond

investing

Bruce C. N. Greenwald, Judd Kahn, Paul D. Sonkin, and Michael van Biema

A practical introduction to valuing businesses through fundamentals rather than market hype. It is most useful for understanding intrinsic value, competitive advantage, and disciplined investing.

Never Split the Difference

negotiation

Chris Voss with Tahl Raz

Useful for difficult conversations, negotiation, and learning how to uncover what the other person actually values. The techniques around calibrated questions and tactical empathy are especially practical.

The Comfort Crisis

resilience

Michael Easter

Useful for understanding how excessive comfort can weaken resilience, attention, and perspective. It makes a strong case for deliberately choosing difficult experiences instead of avoiding them.
